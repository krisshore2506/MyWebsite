Stress Relief Tips

Simple ways to reduce exam stress and stay relaxed

Helpful Stress Management Tips

  • Practice deep breathing exercises regularly
  • Take short breaks between study sessions
  • Listen to calm and relaxing music
  • Maintain proper sleep schedule
  • Stay hydrated and eat healthy food
  • Talk to friends, teachers, or family when feeling stressed

Relaxation Techniques

You can try meditation, stretching exercises, or light physical activities like walking or yoga to refresh your mind.

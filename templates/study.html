Pomodoro Technique

Study for 25 minutes and then take a 5-minute break. This method helps improve concentration and prevents mental fatigue.

Time Management Tips

  • Create a daily study timetable
  • Prioritize difficult subjects first
  • Set achievable study goals
  • Avoid distractions while studying

Revision Techniques

  • Revise topics regularly
  • Practice previous year question papers
  • Make short notes and flashcards
  • Explain topics to friends or yourself
